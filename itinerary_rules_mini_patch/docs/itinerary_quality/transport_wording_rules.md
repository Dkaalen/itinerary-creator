# Transport Wording Rules

Transport should be clear, polished, and consistent. Day titles should be short; travel-arrangement bullets and inclusions may use richer wording.

## Rail

Day title:

- `Train to Bergen`
- `Train to Stockholm`

Travel arrangement and inclusions wording:

- `Scenic Train Transfer from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Scenic Train Transfer from [origin] to [destination], via [connection]`

Examples:

- `Scenic Train Transfer from Oslo to Bergen — 2:25 PM - 9:33 PM`
- `Scenic Train Transfer from Copenhagen to Stockholm, via Malmö`

Multi-leg rail rules:

- Use the final destination as the day title, not an intermediate connection.
- Preserve the connection in the bullet if useful.
- Preserve important details such as first class tickets.

Example:

`Scenic Train Transfer from Copenhagen to Stockholm, via Malmö`

with details:

- `Depart Copenhagen: 1:59 PM`
- `Change in Malmö`
- `Arrive Stockholm: 7:35 PM`
- `First class tickets included`

Train rows pasted as activity rows should still be treated as transport if they are point-to-point rail routes.

## Coach / bus

Use:

- `Coach Transfer from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Long-distance Coach Transfer from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Panoramic Coach Transfer from [origin] to [destination]` when source says panoramic/scenic.

Examples:

- `Panoramic Coach Transfer from Tromsø to Alta — 4:00 PM - 10:20 PM`
- `Long-distance Coach Transfer from Tromsø to Alta`

If tickets are excluded or to be bought on site, show this as a not-included note and carry it to exclusions.

## Flights

Included flight:

- `Flight from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Flight from [origin] to [destination], via [connection]`

Examples:

- `Flight from Stockholm to Kirkenes, via Oslo — 4:10 PM - 8:30 PM`

Self-arranged flight:

- `Self-arranged flight from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Self-arranged flight to [destination]` if origin is unknown.

Self-arranged flights must not appear in inclusions. They must appear in day logistics and exclusions.

## Cruises and ferries

Day title:

- `Cruise to Bergen`
- `Cruise to Ålesund`
- `Cruise arrival to Bergen`

Commercial wording:

- `Coastal Cruise from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Overnight Coastal Cruise from [origin] to [destination]`
- `Coastal Cruise from [origin] to [destination] onboard [ship name]`

Examples:

- `Overnight Coastal Cruise from Bergen to Ålesund — departs 8:30 PM; arrives 9:45 AM; Polar Outside cabin included`
- `Coastal Cruise from Kirkenes to Bergen onboard MC Havila Castor`

Cabin and meal details should be preserved when present:

- `Balcony Suite and full pension meal plan included`
- `Polar Outside cabin included`

Cruise leisure days are not commercial inclusions.

## Private transfers

Use clear origin/destination language.

Examples:

- `Private transfer from Oslo Airport to your accommodation`
- `Private transfer from your hotel to Ålesund Airport`
- `Private transfer from your hotel to Copenhagen Central Station`
- `Private transfer from Kirkenes Cruise Harbor to your accommodation`
- `Private transfer to your accommodation in Copenhagen`

Avoid repeated vague lines in long inclusions. For long itineraries, grouped wording can be better:

`Private transfers between airports, stations, cruise ports and accommodations as listed in the itinerary`

## Self transfers

Use:

- `Self transfer from [origin] to [destination]`

Examples:

- `Self transfer from your hotel to the bus station`
- `Self transfer from the bus station to your accommodation`

Hard rule:

Self transfers must never appear in inclusions.

Self transfers should appear as day logistics and be covered in exclusions.

## Transfer cleanup rules

Common raw source phrases and desired output:

- `Private Airport to Hotel` → `Private transfer from the airport to your accommodation`
- `Private Hotel to Airport` → `Private transfer from your hotel to the airport`
- `Private Hotel to Station` → `Private transfer from your hotel to the station`
- `Private Station to Hotel` → `Private transfer from the station to your accommodation`
- `Private Hotel to Cruise Harbor` → `Private transfer from your hotel to the cruise harbor`

If city context is available, include it when helpful.
